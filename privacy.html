Our consultation request form does not send or store anything on this website. It opens your email app with your request filled in, and nothing reaches us until you press Send. If you arrived through a tagged link, the form passes those link tags (source, medium, campaign, and content, when present) along with the request. If you call us, email us, or send a request through our consultation form, we use what you send to respond to you and to check for conflicts of interest. If you do not hire us, we keep only your name, the names of the other people or businesses involved, the date, and a short description of the matter so we can check for conflicts. We keep that information confidential and do not sell it. Please do not send confidential details by email until we confirm we can help, because others could see those messages.
