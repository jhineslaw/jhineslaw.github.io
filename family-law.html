Family law in Roane, Morgan, and Loudon Counties

Divorce, custody and parenting plans, child support, adoption, and orders of protection. If you and the other side agree, most matters are handled for one set fee. If the case is contested, you get a written fee estimate: a starting retainer plus a flat fee for each hearing or other event that happens.

What to expect

  1. Consultation. We talk through your situation, whether it's agreed or contested, and the likely steps in your county's court.
  2. Written quote and agreement. You get the exact fee in writing. Payment plans are available: for set fees, 50% down and the balance within 90 days.
  3. Filing and court dates. We prepare and file the papers and keep you updated on each date.

Common questions

What if my agreed divorce becomes contested?

We'll tell you in writing before any contested work starts, and your agreement explains how the fee changes.

Do court costs come out of the set fee?

No. Court costs and filing fees are separate from attorney fees.

Can I get help with just part of my case?

Yes. We can help with one part of your case, such as document review, document preparation, or a single court appearance. Any court document we prepare will say "Prepared by Jason R. Hines, Attorney at Law, BPR No. 028139," and will be filed with a Notice of Limited Appearance.
