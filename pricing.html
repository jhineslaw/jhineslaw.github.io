Fees and pricing

Jason R. Hines, Attorney at Law, responsible attorney. Office: Harriman, TN.

We post our fees so you know what to expect before you hire us. Every case is quoted in writing after a consultation. All fees below are attorney fees plus court costs and filing fees.

Fees do not include court filing fees, service of process fees, recording fees, or other case costs as needed. We will give you a clear written explanation of expected charges before they are incurred.

How our fees work

  • Set fee: one price for the work listed. Used for agreed (uncontested) matters and General Sessions criminal cases.
  • Fee estimate: used for contested cases and Criminal Court cases. You pay a starting retainer, plus a flat fee for each hearing, deposition, mediation, or trial day that happens. Typical totals below are estimates. Your case may differ.
  • Event fees and date changes: Event fees (hearings, depositions, mediation, trial days) are paid ahead into our trust account. Half reserves the date and is nonrefundable. If the date has to move because of an emergency, because of me, or because the court or the other side reschedules it, what you paid carries over to the new date (one narrow exception for last-minute moves by the court or the other side is in your agreement). If you ask to move it, a new reservation fee is due. Your written agreement explains the details.
  • Consultations: Free 30-minute phone consultation. Immigration appointments are held by video. We'll tell you about any fee before we schedule.
  • Payment plans: available. For set fees, 50% down and the balance within 90 days. For contested cases, a payment schedule we agree on in writing.
  • Late payments: If a payment is more than 14 days late, we will send you written notice.
  • Interest: We do not charge interest on payment plans.
  • File retention: We keep client files for at least five years after the representation ends.

Veterans, service members, first responders, and their widows and widowers

10% off our attorney fees, including hourly work, for the groups named below.

The 10% applies only to attorney fees. There is no dollar cap. It does not apply to court costs, filing fees, USCIS or other government fees, recording fees or transfer tax, travel costs, expense deposits, the $75 signing fee, or other third-party costs.

We check proof of status when you hire us: any ID or document showing it, such as a DD-214, VA card, or department ID or letter. Not sure whether you qualify? Ask us before you sign. Payment plans are still available. The fee in your signed written agreement controls.

I am the son and grandson of veterans. They hold a close place in my heart. That is why we give a 10% discount on our attorney fees, including hourly work, to veterans (including military retirees), active-duty military, and Reserve and National Guard members.

The same discount is our way of thanking first responders: police, firefighters and EMTs (paid or volunteer), 911 dispatchers, corrections officers, and retired first responders. It also extends to widows and widowers of veterans, service members, and first responders.

The terms above explain what the 10% applies to and what it does not. The discount is a thank-you; it doesn't change the work we do for you.

Remote signing (online notarization)

  • Signing fee (includes notary): $75 for one signer in a standard remote signing session, including the online platform cost. This includes a notarial fee of not more than $25 per notarization, as Tennessee law allows; the rest covers our signing service (preparing your files, scheduling, and checking the notary certificate). Additional signers, seals, and witnesses are passed through at cost.
  • Not available for wills or codicils. Tennessee requires two witnesses who sign in the presence of the person making the will, so wills are signed in person.
  • We use in-person signing instead when we have any concern about a signer's understanding or free choice, including many life estate deeds for older clients.
  • We confirm first that the county register, lender, title company, court, or out-of-state or foreign recipient will accept an online-notarized document. You will need an unexpired government photo ID and must pass an online identity quiz.

Limited-scope help

We can help with one part of your case, such as document review, document preparation, or a single court appearance. We quote the fee in writing before we start.

When we prepare court documents for you to file on your own, our help is always disclosed to the court:

  • Every document we prepare says "Prepared by Jason R. Hines, Attorney at Law, BPR No. 028139."
  • Each filing comes with a Notice of Limited Appearance telling the court that we are involved only to prepare documents and that you have agreed to that limited role in writing.
  • Before we start, you sign a short written acknowledgment of what we will and will not do. Unless we agree in writing to do more, you handle filing, service, negotiation, and court dates.
