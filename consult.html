No-cost consultation if a court has found you indigent

If a Tennessee court has already found you indigent in your case (for example, by appointing a lawyer for you) and you would like to speak with a private attorney, your first 30-minute consultation is free. After you first contact us, please bring or send a copy of the court's order or indigency determination before or at the consultation. If you hire us, our regular fees apply.
